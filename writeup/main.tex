\section*{Approach}
The model is a retrieval-augmented hierarchical classifier on an ImageNet-pretrained
EfficientNet-B0 backbone (\texttt{efficientnet\_b0.ra4\_e3600\_r224\_in1k} via \texttt{timm}),
under the 5{,}000{,}000-parameter budget (final: \textbf{4{,}930{,}570} params, all counted --
pretrained weights are not frozen). Five views per image (one global crop, four 64\%-width
corner crops) pass through the \emph{shared} backbone and are fused. Heads on the fused
feature: a 12-way country classifier; \emph{four} independent country-scoped geo-cell
classifiers (CPlaNet-style: four separately-seeded recursive kd-tree partitions of the same
training points, each cell belonging to one country by construction), sharing a single
low-rank projection so many fine cells fit the budget; a local-offset regressor; and a
224-d L2-normalized embedding. Inference does not arg-max the model's own cells: it scores
\emph{every labelled training image} by
$\log P(\text{country}) + \tfrac14\sum_k \log P(\text{cell}_k\mid\text{country}) + w\cdot
\text{cosine similarity}$, then decodes the coordinate as a density-weighted spherical mean
over the strongest coherent geographic mode among the top candidates. Training combines
\emph{haversine-smoothed} cell cross-entropy (below), country cross-entropy, offset Huber
loss, a smooth geo-distance-matching loss on the embedding, a five-view consistency loss,
and a supervised geographic contrastive loss.

\section*{What was tried}
An iterative, evidence-driven search on fixed group-stratified 5-fold splits (500\,m
location clusters kept whole; honest fixed-decoder median km).

\textbf{Architecture progression.} Direct-regression MobileViT-XS (450--500\,km, underfit)
$\to$ hierarchical country+single-cell EfficientNet-B0 (193\,km) $\to$ $K$ independent coarse
cell partitions combined at decode time, following CPlaNet's argument that several coarse
views triangulate a finer location without needing enough data for a finer joint class
space (183\,km at $K$=2) $\to$ shared multi-view (global+4 local) retrieval against the full
training gallery with mode-aware spherical decoding (155\,km) $\to$ + supervised
geo-contrastive loss (149.5\,km, the previous submission's recipe). Backbone swaps
(FastViT-T8, RepViT, StarNet, MobileViT-XS@384) all matched or lost.

\textbf{The oracle analysis that redirected everything.} Assuming \emph{perfect} cell
classification and predicting the cell's training-point centroid, the fold-0 median would be
85\,km with the 96-cell partitions actually used, \textbf{53\,km at 192 cells, 33\,km at 368
cells}. The model's measured cell top-1 accuracy was only 43\%. The gap to a good score was
therefore \emph{cell granularity and classification accuracy}, not a fundamental
``information ceiling'' as the previous writeup concluded. Two changes followed.
\emph{(i)~Finer cells}: 96\,$\to$\,368 cells per partition ($\sim$26 training images/cell),
with a shared low-rank trunk before the four cell heads to stay under budget. On its own this
was roughly neutral --- the 368-way classifier tops out at 13\% top-1, so its precision gain
is cancelled by its accuracy loss. \emph{(ii)~Haversine label smoothing} (PIGEON, Haas et
al.\ 2024): the target for cell $i$ is $\text{softmax}(-\,\text{haversine}(\text{centroid}_i,
\text{truth})/\tau)$ over that image's country's cells, $\tau\!\approx\!45$\,km (tuned to
$\sim$1.7$\times$ the median inter-cell spacing). Neighbouring cells share the label mass, so
the classifier is rewarded for landing in the right \emph{area}. Combined:
\textbf{192 cells $+$ smoothing = 148.5\,km}; \textbf{368 cells $+$ smoothing $+$ a longer
55-epoch schedule = 137.2\,km} (fold 0), 132.7\,km after a decoder-hyperparameter sweep
(higher retrieval weight, 150\,km mode radius). A one-time correction from the provided
\texttt{orientation\_flags.json} (rotate the $\sim$9\% of images flagged upside-down; the
previous pipeline ignored this file) is kept --- neutral on CV, but the flag also covers the
holdout set where the model cannot adapt.

\textbf{Per-country diagnosis and negative results.} A per-country breakdown of the 137\,km
model splits sharply: Iceland/Belarus/Finland/Norway 52--96\,km, but \textbf{France 464\,km
at 58\% country accuracy}, Italy 363, Germany 273, Spain 311, Poland 205. When France is
wrong it is \emph{confidently} wrong (not in the model's top-2), so decode-time country
priors and hard top-$k$ country masking did not help; nor did a 2.5$\times$ country loss
weight, region-level contrastive positives (OSV-5M), an MLP country head, 384\,px input,
tighter local crops, or a GeoCLIP-style continuous location encoder on the shared embedding
(the auxiliary alignment loss dragged the retrieval embedding). Rural continental Europe is
genuinely ambiguous from one street-level frame at this data volume.

\textbf{What did move it: ensembling.} Fusing several models \emph{in gallery-score space}
(CPlaNet Eq.~3, not by averaging coordinates as an earlier failed attempt did): a 4-model
score-space ensemble reaches \textbf{122.3\,km} on fold 0 --- the mean error drops from 427
to 368\,km because a majority of diverse models rarely all misplace the same French image
into Germany. The submitted model uses the five CV-fold models fused this way [FINAL NUMBERS

\section*{Final model and honest evaluation}
The final recipe --- $K$=4 $\times$ 368 haversine-smoothed cells, shared low-rank cell trunk,
224-d contrastive embedding, orientation correction, 55-epoch cosine schedule --- was fixed
from the fold-0 screen, then trained once per CV fold. Reported number is the median of the
five fold medians with a single fixed decoder configuration (not per-fold cherry-picked).
Predictions for the holdout are the score-space ensemble of the five fold models.

\section*{Limitations and what we'd try with more budget}
(1) A dedicated France/Germany/Italy/Poland ``continental cluster'' specialist model as an
ensemble member, gated by the country head. (2) Per-country-adaptive cell granularity ---
finer cells only where data density supports them. (3) A stronger sub-5M backbone with an
explicitly decoupled country vs.\ cell representation (GeoGuessNet's query-based heads).
(4) Test-time augmentation beyond horizontal flip (multi-scale, the four native-resolution
corner crops).
